Our Services

Expert Audio Post-Production

At Suji Sound, we offer a full spectrum of audio post-production services designed to elevate your content and enhance your audience’s experience.

Dubbing

We provide professional dubbing services in multiple languages, ensuring your content resonates with global audiences. Our expert voice actors, directors, and sound engineers work together to match tone, timing, and emotion.

Lyric Writing & Translation

We offer creative lyric writing and professional translation services for songs across different languages and cultures. Our team ensures your message resonates clearly and artistically with your audience.

Voice Over

From commercials and corporate videos to animations and e-learning materials, we offer clear, expressive, and impactful voice-over recordings that captivate your audience.

Music Editing

Precise and creative music editing to match the rhythm, mood, and timing of your project. We ensure seamless transitions and a polished, cohesive sound tailored to your needs.

Mixing

Our audio mixing services balance every sonic element — dialogue, music, sound effects — into a cohesive and immersive soundscape tailored to the medium.

Sound Design

We create unique and expressive soundscapes that enhance storytelling. From atmospheric textures to high-impact effects, we add depth and character to your content.

Jingle Production

We compose catchy and memorable jingles that leave a lasting impression. Crafted to reflect your brand identity and engage your target audience through sound.

Dialogue Editing

Cleaning and balancing of spoken tracks for maximum clarity. We remove unwanted noise and ensure every word is heard exactly as intended.

ADR

Specializing in precise dialogue replacement and synchronization for film and TV. Our ADR sessions ensure perfect timing and natural delivery.

Audio Description

Making visual content accessible to visually impaired audiences. We craft detailed narration describing key visual elements without disrupting the original experience.
